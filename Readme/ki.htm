Known Issues

- Video cards with the Riva 128 chipset should use reference drivers. You can download the reference driver for Riva 128-based cards from www.nvidia.com.

- Owners of Voodoo II-based cards should set Texture Quality/Performance to Performance for best results.

- The following is a list of video card chipsets that are not supported:

I740
Power VR (PCX2)
Rage II
Rage Pro (4MB versions only)
Verite 1000L-P
Verite 2100
Virge
Voodoo Rush

G400 - (Driver Issue. May be supported with a future driver update from Matrox)

To find out which chipset you have...

  1. Click , "Find", "Files and Folders"...
  2. In the "Named" box type "DXDIAG.EXE". Make sure that the "Look in" window says "My Computer" and click on button. When Dxdiag.exe comes up Double click on the Icon.
  3. Click on the "Display" or "Display 1" tab. In the Device section it will list your chipset as "Chip Type", and the version of that driver is listed under "Version" in the Drivers section.
